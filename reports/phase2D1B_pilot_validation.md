# Phase 2D.1B-Pilot - Validation

## Scientific Positioning and Scope

This is a DICOM metadata-aware, standard-aligned *reference representation* pipeline. Phase 2D.1B does **not** prove that this representation produces the best downstream detection performance; claiming superiority over alternative preprocessing strategies would require controlled downstream evidence. Whether a controlled downstream ablation is included remains pending mentor confirmation and is not part of the Phase 2D.1B Definition of Done.

Geometry preservation here refers to unchanged pixel-matrix dimensions and ordering (no crop/resize/rotation/flip/transpose/EXIF orientation). It does not claim independent validation of patient-space orientation.
